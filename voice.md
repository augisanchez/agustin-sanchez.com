# Voice profile: the Bourdain register

How Agustin's site copy should sound. Reference: Anthony Bourdain's written voice
(Kitchen Confidential, Medium Raw, the New Yorker piece that started it all,
Parts Unknown narration). This is a tone profile, not an impersonation. The goal
is Agustin's ideas carried with Bourdain's delivery instincts. Nobody should read
the site and think "Bourdain cosplay." They should think "this guy talks like a
person, and he's clearly been in the kitchen."

---

## Why Bourdain works as the reference

Bourdain wrote like a line cook who happened to have read everything: total
authority earned through scars, zero interest in performing authority. He was
romantic about craft and merciless about pretension, including his own. That's
the exact posture for a design leader's site: someone who can still make the
thing, has opinions because he's paid for them, and refuses to sound like a
LinkedIn post.

## The mechanics (what actually makes it sound like him)

**1. The long roll, then the punch.**
His signature rhythm: a sentence that accumulates clauses, piling up specifics,
gathering speed, and then a short one. Sometimes a fragment. The short sentence
does the killing.
> Roll: "We built the design system from the codebase, matched every token to
> what engineering was actually living with, and shipped designs back as working
> UI code." Punch: "Nobody waited."

**2. Self-implication before judgment.**
He earns the right to criticize by indicting himself first. Never "teams make
this mistake." Always "I've made this mistake, here's what it cost." The
confession is the credential.

**3. Specificity is the style.**
No "delicious," no "innovative." Proper nouns, numbers, exact details, trade
terms. 208 components. 135 gigabytes. The 11-chip filter row. Bourdain never
said the food was amazing; he told you it was oxtail, and who made it, and what
her kitchen smelled like. Adjectives are what you use when you weren't there.

**4. High-low collision.**
Erudite word, then street word, in the same sentence. "The epistemology of it
was simple: open the damn repo." The register drop is the humor. Used sparingly,
it's electric. Used constantly, it's shtick.

**5. Direct address.**
He grabs the reader by the collar: "you," rhetorical questions, commands.
"Look at the middle screen." "You've seen this meeting." It assumes the reader
is smart and busy.

**6. Anti-pretension as a value, not a pose.**
He deflates marketing language on contact, including his own. If a sentence
would survive in a corporate deck, it dies here. The tell: any sentence that
could describe anyone. Bourdain sentences could only describe the specific
thing in front of him.

**7. Love expressed through knowledge.**
When he praised, it was never gush; it was detail. Reverence = knowing how the
thing is actually made and saying so plainly. This is how the site should talk
about craft, photography, and the people in the case studies.

**8. Dignity of the line.**
His deepest theme: the people who do the actual work, the dishwashers and
line cooks, are the honor of the trade. Translate: engineers, early-career
designers, editors, research participants. Credit flows down, blame flows up
(to himself).

**9. Committed opinions, no hedging.**
Never "I think maybe we should consider." He commits, and if he's wrong later
he says that plainly too. Confidence isn't volume; it's the absence of
qualifier words. Kill: "quite," "fairly," "arguably," "in many ways."

**10. Dark-dry humor, aimed upward or inward.**
The joke targets himself, the powerful, or the absurdity of the system. Never
the client, never the team, never the user.

## The dials (calibration per surface)

Bourdain at 100% is profanity, heroin, and meat. We run him at 30-50%, blended
into the register already locked in copy-final.md (short declarative, first
person, sentence case).

| Surface | Dial | What that means |
|---|---|---|
| Overview / hero | 30% | Rhythm + commitment. No jokes in headlines. |
| Design Direction (POV) | 40% | Self-implication, the roll-and-punch, direct address. |
| Case studies | 40% | Specificity engine on full. War-story cadence in Approach sections. Outcomes stay dry. |
| Photography / Outliers | 50% | Most atmosphere, most sensory detail, most affection. |
| Contact / chrome | 10% | Clean and warm. Nobody wants a monologue on a form. |

## Hard guardrails

- **No profanity.** The energy comes from rhythm and specificity, not the words
  he could get away with. If a sentence needs "damn" to land, it can have it,
  once per page, maybe.
- **No food metaphors.** The fastest way to turn homage into parody.
- **No borrowed catchphrases or biography.** Nothing that imitates his actual
  lines or trades on him. He's the reference, not the brand.
- **No nihilism.** Bourdain's darkness was his; this site is fundamentally
  optimistic about making things. Keep the skepticism, lose the shadow.
- **House rules still govern:** no em dashes (his beloved dash becomes a period
  or a comma; the punch survives, and honestly improves), no comma splices, no
  corporate filler, sentence case.
- **Facts stay sacred.** The voice never outruns the evidence. Client-reported
  numbers keep their attribution. Projections stay labeled.

## Proof: before / after on real site copy

**Design Direction, Leadership movement (current):**
> "Most of my work is setting direction: shaping product strategy, framing the
> problem, and helping a team decide what's actually worth building."

**Bourdain register:**
> "Here's what they actually pay me for. Not the mockups. Walking into a room
> where everyone is politely agreeing about the wrong problem, and asking the
> question that stops the meeting."

**Healing Track, method section (current):**
> "The usual way, design builds a cathedral in Figma and engineering builds a
> second cathedral that sort of resembles it. Two sources of truth, endless
> reconciliation."

**Bourdain register:**
> "You know how this usually goes. Design spends three months building a
> cathedral in Figma. Engineering looks at it, sighs, and builds a different
> cathedral out of what's actually in the pantry. Two sources of truth, both
> lying. We skipped it. I opened the repo first and built the design system
> out of what was already true."

**The Outliers (current):**
> "The method is simple. Ride. Meet. Document. Share."

**Bourdain register:**
> "The method is not sophisticated. Ride somewhere. Talk to whoever's there.
> Point the camera at what you'd want to remember. It turns out a motorcycle
> is the best icebreaker ever invented: nobody asks what you do for a living
> when they want to know what you're riding."

## The test

Read the paragraph out loud. If you can't hear a specific human being slightly
too honest at a bar at 1am, it's not there yet. If you can hear him doing a
bit, it's gone too far. The target is the morning-after version: same honesty,
sober, still funny.
