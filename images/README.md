# Photos for the site

Drop your photos in `images/incoming/` (any filenames, any size, JPEG/PNG/HEIC/WebP all fine). Then tell me which project each belongs to and I'll do the rest: resize to web sizes, strip EXIF, name them sensibly, and wire them into the right pages.

## What I need, roughly

| Where | How many | Best orientation | Notes |
|-------|----------|------------------|-------|
| Overview marquee | 8–14 | mixed | The looping strip on the home page. Variety is good. |
| Overview "About" | 1 | square-ish or portrait | The studio/portrait shot beside the intro. |
| The Photographic Journal (lead) | 1 | landscape | Plus a few more if you want a small gallery. |
| The Inbetween (lead + gallery) | 1 + 4–8 | mixed | This is the photography project, so more is better. |
| The Outliers (lead + gallery) | 1 + 4–8 | mixed | Your own Outliers shots (not the moodboard refs). |

## Good source sizes
Originals are fine. I'll export display versions around 1600–2400px on the long edge at ~80% quality so the pages stay fast. You don't need to resize anything yourself.
