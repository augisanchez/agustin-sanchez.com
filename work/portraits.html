Portraits

Photography Ongoing

Faces, mostly. People I know well, people I'd met an hour earlier, people deciding in real time whether to trust the guy with the camera. That negotiation is the picture.

Portraiture is the shortest route to the thing I care about in every discipline: paying attention to a person until they show you something true. The camera doesn't do that part. It just makes the attention visible, and holds the receipt.
