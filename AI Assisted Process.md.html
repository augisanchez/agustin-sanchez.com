# AI-Amplified Product Delivery

## Signal-Based Decision System + Coordinated Product Trios


---


## 🧭 Core POV


We don’t scale by adding more people to a single team.  

We scale by running **multiple small, focused teams in parallel**, coordinated through shared signals.


> Build while learning. Validate while moving. Decide with signal.


---


## ⚡ Key Principles


- **Small, autonomous teams (Product Trios)**

- **Parallel workstreams, not linear phases**

- **Continuous validation (not a single step)**

- **AI accelerates insight, not just output**

- **Decisions are driven by signal, not progress**


---


## 🧠 Signal-Based Decision System


We validate three signals continuously:


| Signal | Question | Owner |

|--------|----------|-------|

| **Desirability** | Do users want this? | Design + Research |

| **Feasibility** | Can we build this? | Engineering |

| **Viability** | Should we build this? | Product + Leadership |


---


### ⚡ Critical Decision Moments


#### 1. Viability Filter (Week 1–2)

- Applied during Diverge

- Filters out misaligned or low-value ideas early


#### 2. Decision Gate (Week 4–6)

- Applied before MVP build lock

- Combines all three signals

- Output: Go / Adjust / Kill


---


## 🔁 Parallel Delivery Model (6–8 Weeks)


Work happens across **two coordinated lanes**:


- Product / Design / Research

- Engineering


Validation is embedded throughout.


---


## 🎨 Product, Design & Research Lane


| Phase | Focus | Core Activities | Validation (Desirability) | Outputs |

|------|------|----------------|---------------------------|---------|

| Align & Frame | Build POV | Research synthesis, stakeholder alignment, hypothesis framing | Assumption checks | Opportunity map, hypotheses |

| Diverge | Explore | Concept generation, flows, rapid iteration | Concept testing | Concept directions, early signals |

| Converge | Refine | Prototypes, interaction models | Usability testing | Validated prototypes |

| Deep Validation | Pressure test | Iteration, refinement | Behavioral testing | Evidence-backed UX |

| MVP | Finalize | UX refinement, launch support | Live user signals | MVP experience |


---


## ⚙️ Engineering Lane


| Phase | Focus | Core Activities | Validation (Feasibility) | Outputs |

|------|------|----------------|--------------------------|---------|

| Align & Frame | Understand system | Technical discovery, constraints | System validation | Constraint map |

| Diverge | Explore feasibility | Spikes, architecture direction | Feasibility validation | Architecture direction |

| Converge | Build | Parallel build begins, core services | Implementation validation | Working components |

| Deep Validation | Stabilize | Integration, edge cases, performance | System validation | Stable MVP |

| MVP | Deliver | Release, instrumentation | Real-world system validation | Live MVP |


---


## 🤖 AI Amplification (Across All Phases)


- Synthesizes large amounts of data and research

- Generates hypotheses and concept variations

- Accelerates prototyping and development

- Analyzes feedback and identifies patterns

- Surfaces insights from live product signals


> AI reduces time to insight, not just time to output.


---


## 🔗 Coordination Model: Product Trios


Each **Product Trio (Pod)** consists of:

- Product

- Design

- Engineering


Each trio:

- Owns a focused problem space

- Runs the full 6–8 week cycle

- Produces validated outputs


---


### 🧩 System Structure


#### Oversight Layer

- Head of Product

- Design Director

- Tech Lead


**Role:**

- Strategic alignment

- Signal synthesis

- Prioritization


---


#### Trio Layer (3–5 Pods)


Each pod:

- Operates independently

- Shares learnings across system

- Contributes to overall direction


---


#### Key Principle


> Pods operate independently, but learn collectively.


---


## 🔁 Coordination Points


| Moment | Coordination |

|--------|-------------|

| Week 0–1 | Product framing ↔ Engineering constraints |

| Week 1–2 | Design exploration ↔ Feasibility spikes |

| Week 2–4 | Prototyping ↔ Parallel build |

| Week 4–6 | User validation ↔ System integration |

| Week 6–8 | MVP launch ↔ Shared learning |
