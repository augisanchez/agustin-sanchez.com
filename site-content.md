## Section 3 — Leadership

*Background: Mint / Light*
*Section label: Leadership*

### Headline
DESIGN IS A TRANSLATION PROBLEM.

### Body
Design's defensible value has never been the artifact. It's the ability to translate — between what users need, what engineering can build, and what the business can sustain — in a way that drives decisions rather than debates. AI changes the tools. It doesn't change what the work is.

That judgment isn't innate — it's accumulated. From early-stage bets where the right frame changed everything, to enterprise-scale systems where alignment was the actual work. The range is the point. And it compounds.

## Section 5 — Principles

*Background: Paper / Light*
*Section label: Principles*

### Headline
What I believe.

---

### Principle: Craft

**Headline:**
The tools will change. Your craft doesn't have to.

**Body:**
The question has never been how fast you can produce — it's whether you know which one is worth keeping. Craft isn't about execution speed. It's the ability to recognize the right answer when you're looking at it. That's the one thing that doesn't get compressed away.

---

### Principle: Courage

**Headline:**
THE BEST ANSWERS LIVE IN UNCOMFORTABLE PLACES.

**Body:**
The real answer usually requires someone to say something that makes the room go quiet. That discomfort is signal. Courage mostly looks like precision: being specific about what's true when no one else will be.

### Panel A — Belief statement

Where belief becomes practice.

**Subhead:**
AI compresses the cycle. Not the thinking.

**Body:**
Most delivery failures are signal failures — teams building toward an answer that was never validated. AI-amplified delivery runs the same rigor at a fraction of the cost. Each phase answers a question before the next one begins.

---

### Panel B — Signal-Based Decision System

**Label:** Signal Based Decision System

**Headline:**
5 Phases. 3 Signals. 2 Gates.

**Body:**
Each phase answers a specific question before the next begins — compressing cycle time without compressing the thinking.

**Framework:**

| # | Phase | Intent |
|---|-------|--------|
| 01 | Align & Frame | Establish informed starting point |
| 02 | Diverge | ⚡ Viability Filter — eliminate weak ideas early |
| 03 | Converge | Strengthen best options |
| 04 | Deep Validation | ⚡ Decision Gate — Go / Adjust / Kill |
| 05 | MVP & Live Signal | Inform next iteration |
